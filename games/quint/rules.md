# Quint

**A dice game invented by Cole**

Roll, keep and reroll the dice until all five match. Complete the challenge in fewer rolls than the other players to score a point.

## What you need

- 5 standard six-sided dice
- 2 or more players
- Paper and a pencil for keeping score

## Aim of the game

Try to roll **five dice showing the same number** in as few rolls as possible.

## How to play a round

Each player takes a turn with all five dice.

1. Roll all five dice. This is **Roll 1**.
2. Choose any dice to keep.
3. Reroll any dice you want to change, then increase your roll count: Roll 2, Roll 3 and so on.
4. After every roll, you may choose different dice to keep. You may also reroll dice that you kept earlier.
5. Continue until all five dice show the same number.
6. Write down how many rolls you needed.

There is no limit to the number of rolls in a turn.

## Example turn

| Roll | Dice | Choice |
|:----:|:-----|:-------|
| 1 | 2, 2, 3, 5, 6 | Keep the two 2s. |
| 2 | 2, 2, 4, 4, 4 | Change your target and keep the three 4s. |
| 3 | 4, 4, 4, 1, 4 | Keep the four 4s. |
| 4 | 4, 4, 4, 4, 4 | Quint completed in 4 rolls. |

## Scoring a round

After every player has completed a turn, compare the roll counts.

- The player who used the fewest rolls wins the round and scores **1 point**.
- If two or more players tie for the fewest rolls, each tied player scores **1 point**.

## Winning the game

A game lasts **five rounds**. After the fifth round, add up the points.

- The player with the most points wins.
- If two or more players share the highest score, the game is a **draw** and they share the win.
