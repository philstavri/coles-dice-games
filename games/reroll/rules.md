# Reroll

> **Reroll — invented by Cole**

Reroll is a quick, family-friendly dice game about choosing which dice to keep and which to risk. Every turn gives you one chance to reroll, so choose carefully and aim for the highest score.

This specification includes two game modes:

1. **Reroll** — every turn score is added to a running total.
2. **Reroll Rounds** — the highest score in each round earns a point.

## Players and equipment

- **Players:** 2 or more
- **Dice:** Five standard six-sided dice (d6)
- **Also useful:** Paper and a pencil, or another way to record scores

## Game length

Before playing, choose one game length:

- **Short game:** 5 rounds
- **Long game:** 10 rounds

A **round** is complete after every player has taken one turn.

## Setup

1. Choose whether to play **Reroll** or **Reroll Rounds**.
2. Choose a short game (5 rounds) or a long game (10 rounds).
3. Choose the first player by any agreed method.
4. Play continues in the same player order for every round.
5. Prepare a score sheet for cumulative totals or round points, depending on the chosen mode.

## Turn sequence

On your turn:

1. **Roll all five dice.**
2. **Choose which dice to keep.** You may keep any number of dice, including none or all five.
3. **Reroll once, if applicable.** Reroll every die you did not keep. All chosen dice are rerolled together, and you must accept the new results. You cannot reroll again.
4. **Calculate your turn score.** Add the final values of all five dice.

If you keep all five dice after the first roll, your turn ends without a reroll. Your first roll is your final result.

The lowest possible turn score is **5** and the highest is **30**.

## Mode 1: Reroll

In **Reroll**, players build a cumulative total across the whole game.

1. After each turn, add the player's turn score to their cumulative total.
2. Continue until every player has taken one turn in each of the chosen 5 or 10 rounds.
3. The player with the highest cumulative total wins.

### Final tie

If two or more players tie for the highest cumulative total, only those tied players take part in a tiebreak round. Each tied player takes one normal turn, and the player with the highest tiebreak turn score wins.

If the highest tiebreak score is tied, only the players tied on that highest score play another tiebreak round. Repeat until exactly one player has the highest tiebreak score.

Tiebreak scores do not change the players' cumulative totals.

### Example

Maya rolls **2, 4, 5, 6, 6**. She keeps **5, 6, 6** and rerolls the **2** and **4**. The new dice are **3** and **4**, so her final dice are **3, 4, 5, 6, 6**.

Her turn score is:

`3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 6 = 24`

Maya adds **24** to her cumulative total.

## Mode 2: Reroll Rounds

In **Reroll Rounds**, players compete for 1 point in each round.

1. Every player takes one turn.
2. Compare all turn scores for that round.
3. The player with the highest turn score receives **1 point**.
4. If multiple players tie for the highest turn score, **each tied player receives 1 point**. There is no tiebreak within a normal round.
5. Begin the next round and continue until all 5 or 10 rounds are complete.
6. The player with the most points wins.

### Final tie

If two or more players tie for the most points after the final scheduled round, only those tied players take part in a tiebreak round. Each tied player takes one normal turn, and the player with the highest tiebreak turn score wins.

If the highest tiebreak score is tied, only the players tied on that highest score play another tiebreak round. Repeat until exactly one player has the highest tiebreak score.

No points are awarded during final tiebreak rounds; their only purpose is to determine the winner.

### Example

At the end of a round, the scores are:

- Maya: **23**
- Cole: **26**
- Sam: **26**

Cole and Sam share the highest score, so they each receive **1 point**. Maya receives no point. The players then begin the next round; Cole and Sam do not play a round tiebreak.

## Rules at a glance

| Rule | Answer |
|---|---|
| Players | 2 or more |
| Dice | Five standard six-sided dice |
| First roll | Roll all five dice |
| Keep | Keep any number, including none or all five |
| Reroll | Reroll the unkept dice once only |
| Turn score | Add the final values of all five dice |
| Short game | 5 rounds |
| Long game | 10 rounds |
| **Reroll** scoring | Add every turn score to a cumulative total |
| **Reroll** winner | Highest cumulative total after the final round |
| **Reroll Rounds** scoring | Highest turn score in each round earns 1 point; all players tied for highest earn 1 point each |
| **Reroll Rounds** winner | Most points after the final round |
| Final tie | Tied leaders play normal tiebreak turns until one player has the highest score |

## Quick teaching script

“Roll all five dice. Keep whichever dice you want, then reroll the others once. Add all five final dice for your turn score. In **Reroll**, add that score to your total. In **Reroll Rounds**, the best score that round earns a point. After 5 or 10 rounds, the player with the best total or the most points wins.”
