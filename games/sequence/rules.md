# Sequence

**Invented by Cole**

Build the longest run of consecutive numbers. Score points for each turn, and finish with the highest total to win.

## What you need

- 5 standard six-sided dice
- 2 or more players
- Paper and a pencil for keeping score

## How to play

Each player gets **two rolls per turn**.

1. Roll all five dice.
2. Choose any dice to keep. You may keep none, some, or all five.
3. Reroll the other dice once.
4. Score your final five dice.

## How to score

Find the **longest sequence of consecutive, distinct numbers** in your final five dice. A sequence could be **2, 3, 4** or **3, 4, 5, 6**.

- Duplicates are ignored. They do not break a sequence.
- Only the longest sequence scores.
- Do not add shorter sequences together.

| Longest sequence | Score |
|:-----------------|------:|
| 1 or 2 numbers   | 0 points |
| 3 numbers        | 5 points |
| 4 numbers        | 10 points |
| 5 numbers        | 20 points |

## Examples

- **2, 3, 4, 4, 5 = 10 points.** The sequence is 2, 3, 4, 5. Ignore the extra 4.
- **1, 2, 3, 4, 5 = 20 points only.** This is one five-number sequence; no other points are added.
- **1, 2, 4, 5, 6 = 5 points.** The longest sequence is 4, 5, 6.
- **2, 2, 3, 5, 6 = 0 points.** The longest sequences have only two numbers.

## How to win

Choose a target before the game begins:

- **Short game:** 50 points
- **Long game:** 100 points

Reaching or passing the target qualifies a player to win, but the game does not stop immediately. **Finish the round so every player receives the same number of turns.**

After the final round, the player with the highest total score wins. If several players reach or pass the target in that round, the player with the highest total wins.
